About Us

Who we are and what inspires us.

🎯 Mission Statement

Our mission is to create a healthier and more relaxing environment by designing a multi-scent humidifier that lets people choose and enjoy fragrances that suit their mood. We aim to combine technology and wellness in a simple, interactive way.

📖 Project Background

This is a BSIT 21005 Project developed by students passionate about innovation and everyday comfort. By blending programming, electronics, and design, we built a humidifier simulation that demonstrates how scents can be controlled digitally to improve atmosphere and well-being.

👁️ Vision

We envision a future where smart humidifiers are seamlessly integrated into homes, offices, and wellness spaces. By connecting scent technology with IoT and smart home systems, our goal is to create personalized environments that adapt to people’s moods, routines, and health needs.
